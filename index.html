Injera (Ethiopian Flatbread)

A spongy, tangy flatbread made from teff flour.

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups teff flour
  • 3 cups water
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt

Instructions:

  1. Mix teff flour, water, and salt in a large bowl.
  2. Stir until the mixture is smooth and well combined.
  3. Let the batter rest for at least 2 hours.
  4. Heat a griddle or skillet over medium heat.
  5. Pour a small amount of batter onto the griddle.
  6. Cook for about 2 minutes on each side or until bubbles form.
